05 · Challenges Encountered

What made this hard

Real constraints from building a field-ready system on a student timeline.

Outdated map tiles

Mapping tiles for park regions show outdated data, complicating accurate geolocation.

Inadequate game park data

Limited authoritative datasets for park boundaries, species, and infrastructure.

Limited development time

A compressed timeline against the scope of a full multi-role platform.

Own these plainly — a team that names real constraints reads as more credible than one that claims a perfect build.
